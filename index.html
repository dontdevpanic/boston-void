About

ABOUT

Members.

Boston Void emerged from the basements of Boston in 2019, born out of frustration and a refusal to stay silent. We play Hardcore because it's the only honest music left.

Four people. One goal. Make noize that matters. We don't write songs for playlists or algorythms. We write them for the kids in the pit who need something real.
